Editor is free software and not registered with Apple, since that costs a
yearly fee. So macOS warns before the first start that it cannot check the
app. The warning is about the missing registration, not about anything found
in Editor. To open it once:

1. Drag Editor into Applications and double-click it there.
2. Click "Done" on the warning.
3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and click
   "Open Anyway" next to Editor. Confirm with your password.

From then on, Editor opens like any other app.

Without the warning: open Terminal (Cmd + Space, type Terminal, Return),
paste this line and press Return. It installs the same release.

  curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sudo-Oliver/Editor/main/scripts/install.sh | sh

The code, the script and the releases: https://github.com/sudo-Oliver/Editor
