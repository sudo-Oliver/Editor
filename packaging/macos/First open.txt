Opening Editor for the first time
=================================

1. Drag Editor onto the Applications folder in this window.

2. Open Applications and double-click Editor.

Editor is free and not registered with Apple, so the first time macOS
says it cannot check the app for malicious software. That is expected.

On macOS 15 (Sequoia) and later:

3. Click "Done" in the warning.
4. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
5. Scroll down to "Editor was blocked to protect your Mac" and click
   "Open Anyway". Confirm with your Mac password or Touch ID.
6. Confirm once more in the last question.

On macOS 14 and earlier, right-click Editor in Applications, choose
"Open" and click "Open" in the question instead.

macOS asks only once. After that Editor opens like any other app.

For people at home in Terminal, this line does the same in one step:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Editor.app

If Editor ever starts from this window or from Downloads, it offers to
move itself to Applications. Say yes: from there it can update itself.
